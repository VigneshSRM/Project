How verification works

  1. Enter the exact 32-character Prescription ID.
  2. The system checks cancellation, expiry and remaining quantities.
  3. Dispense all or part of the prescription. Each fill is logged with the pharmacist.
  4. Verify the ID again for a later partial fill; the remaining quantities are recalculated.
