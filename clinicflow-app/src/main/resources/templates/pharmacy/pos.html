How the POS works

  1. Scan or type the exact 32-character Prescription ID.
  2. Confirm the medicine quantities. Unit prices are pre-filled from the price list.
  3. Add a discount or tax if needed, choose the payment method and complete the sale.
  4. The receipt is saved, stock is updated, and the sale total is posted to Finance as income automatically.
